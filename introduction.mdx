SBGDS is a documentation platform built for teams who care about developer experience. Whether you're shipping API references, internal wikis, or product guides, SBGDS gives you the tools to write in MDX, organize with a flexible sidebar, embed rich UI components, and publish to a blazing-fast hosted site — all without managing infrastructure.

## What you can do with SBGDS

SBGDS is designed to grow with your team — from a single README to a full-scale developer portal. Here's what you can build:

- **Product documentation** — User-facing guides, tutorials, and how-to articles
- **API references** — Auto-generated from OpenAPI/Swagger specs with live request playgrounds
- **Internal wikis** — Password-protected docs for your engineering or ops teams
- **Changelogs** — Structured release notes with versioning support
- **Multi-language sites** — Localize your docs for a global audience

## Get up and running in three steps

New to SBGDS? Follow this quick onboarding path to go from zero to a live documentation site.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.sbgds.com](https://app.sbgds.com) and create your first project from the dashboard. Choose a name, pick a subdomain, and you're ready to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your content">
    Connect a GitHub repository or use the built-in web editor to start writing MDX pages. Define your sidebar structure in `docs.json` and drop in components wherever you need richer presentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your site">
    Push your changes to your main branch. SBGDS detects the update, builds your site, and publishes it to your custom domain — usually in under 30 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
